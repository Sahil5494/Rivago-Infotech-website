## These are placeholders

They are stock, which means every competitor can licence the same frames.
Rivago's own photography — the Pune floor, the Wilmington and Ayr offices,
partners actually on calls — beats all of it, and it is the one thing no
competitor can copy. Swap them in as they are shot.

Video was requested and is not obtainable from here: the Adobe Stock search
returns zero results for `contentType: "Video"` on every query tried, and the
sandbox proxy refuses every other host. The carousel already accepts video —
see the MEDIA SLOT note at the top of `components/ServiceCarousel.tsx`.
